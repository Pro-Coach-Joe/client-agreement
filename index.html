Freezes are designed for extended situations, not for occasional missed sessions. Short trips and busy weeks are handled inside your active membership using Travel Mode and app-based workouts.

Eligibility: A freeze may be requested only for: (1) Extended Travel — away from in-studio sessions for at least 2 consecutive weeks; (2) Medical — medically unable to participate for at least 2 consecutive weeks (doctor's note may be required); or (3) Exceptional Hardship — at the Business's sole discretion.

How to Request: Submit a written freeze request at least 7 days before the next billing date. Retroactive freezes are not permitted. Account must be current (no past-due balance).

Effect: Approved freezes are granted in 2-week or 4-week blocks. The length of the freeze extends the program end date. A freeze does not erase any past-due amount.

Maximum Freeze Lengths (per rolling 12 months): Travel/Personal — up to 8 weeks in blocks of at least 2 weeks. Medical — up to 12 weeks with documentation.
